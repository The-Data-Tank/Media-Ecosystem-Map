# Media Ecosystem Atlas

View the interactive map here: [https://the-data-tank.github.io/Media-Ecosystem-Map/](https://the-data-tank.github.io/Media-Ecosystem-Map/)

## About the project

The Media Ecosystem Atlas visualises the media ecosystem mapping carried out by [The Data Tank](https://www.datatank.org/) as part of our work on public interest media and generative AI.

The data comes from The Data Tank's own collection.

## Background

After an in-depth engagement process with the media ecosystem in Europe and beyond, [we have shared our recommendations](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/63bd953ecb882b8e559c1614/6a4772df194d4bc5b42e5503_Weaving%20a%20Common%20vision%20report%20-%20June%202026%20(1).pdf).

Developments in generative and General Purpose AI (GPAI) systems have raised concerns about extractive data practices, market concentration by a small number of big tech providers, and pervasive weaknesses in data governance. These have implications for how public interest media, a key cornerstone of our democracies, sustain their content production and protect their integrity.

The Data Tank facilitated an action research project and ecosystem dialogue to convene key stakeholders in the media sector and identify strategies that can leverage the power of small and medium media organisations in Europe vis-à-vis large generative AI providers. The goal is to ensure that the media not only serve the public better across contexts and languages, but also derive fair value from their public interest work.

### Aims

- Mobilising power through convening and tapping into collective intelligence from the current media ecosystem in Europe
- Strengthening the media community by providing knowledge on potential tools and solutions and pathways forward
- Helping media organisations to agree on a roadmap and actions

## The process

To achieve these aims, the process encompassed four activities:

1. An ecosystem mapping (visualised in this atlas)
2. A [comprehensive literature review](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/63bd953ecb882b8e559c1614/6a0b38fae86b009d3027b07e_LitReview%20Media%20and%20GenAI%20DataTank%20May18.pdf)
3. A two-part online dialogue
4. An asynchronous wiki-poll

## Findings and recommendations

The resulting report documents the convening process, the areas of common ground and recommendations for next steps. The results are clear pointers for the media sector in Europe to push a common vision and strengthen public interest media at a critical juncture.

We are grateful to the approximately 120 individuals and represented organisations who engaged in this dialogue. We call on policymakers, funders and the wider media ecosystem to consider these recommendations for next steps of funding, investment, regulation and coalition-building. We also call on media and publishers of all sizes to consider the recommendations on building coalitions, sharing resources and pushing for policies that strengthen the bargaining power of public interest media, including small and independent outlets, over extractive generative AI practices.
